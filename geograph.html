GeoGraph

(Day #1)

Guess a country. The game tells you how many land borders separate it from the MYSTERY COUNTRY.

GeoGraph is an interactive daily geography game where players guess a mystery country using land border graph distances.

Each guess reveals how many land borders separate your selected country from the target. A distance of 0 means you have successfully identified the Mystery Country!

Game Information

  • The game tells you how many land borders separate your guess from the Mystery Country.
  • The Mystery Country has to be connected to more other countries through land borders.
  • Because of this, countries without land neighbours and countries from separated pairs (e.g.: UK), cannot be the Mystery Country.
  • If your guess cannot be connected to the Mystery Country through land borders, the game will write: NO CONNECTION.
  • Multiple game modes are available, including Daily Challenge, Weekly Challenge, and Practice.
  • In Weekly Challenge mode you have only 8 guesses and no hint.
  • GeoGraph saves your game progress and stats locally on your device. We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic. No personal data is collected.
  • Some countries share land borders that many people don't even know about: 🇫🇷 France - 🇧🇷 Brazil, 🇫🇷 France - 🇸🇷 Suriname, 🇫🇷 France - 🇳🇱 Netherlands, 🇪🇸 Spain - 🇲🇦 Morocco, 🇩🇰 Denmark - 🇨🇦 Canada
